- Add a real anchor link, not unlinked text, from the Fressi and BNK footers to stlteknoloji.com. For

  pointing to stlteknoloji.com in the Trendyol and Evidea seller profiles where allowed.

  to use oxyra.com, it belongs to a third party. Link it back to the parent from day one.

MEDIUM, manual verification needed:
- Check and claim listings on İTO, TOBB, TİM sector export lists, Arnavutköy and İstanbul chamber
  directories, Europages, Kompass and manufacturer directories. Standard low-cost high-relevance sources
  for an exporter of this profile, and very likely underused.

MEDIUM, realistic openings:
- Export and trade credential citations through TİM and any sector export union STL belongs to.
- Trade show and B2B marketplace profiles. The scale and export footprint fit manufacturer directories,
  not consumer blogs.
- Press coverage of the 35,000 m² facility or export milestones. A natural angle for Turkish trade press,
  which tends to link.
- Reciprocal links from retailers carrying wexta and from B2B clients as authorized manufacturer links.
